📜 Temple History

Baba Kalondiroy Shiv Temple is one of the spiritual heritage temples located in Naihati village, Paschim Medinipur, West Bengal. The temple is deeply connected with Shiva devotion, local Gajan culture, Maha Shivratri celebrations, and traditional village religious gatherings. The ancient temple structure represents the cultural identity and devotional unity of the surrounding region.
