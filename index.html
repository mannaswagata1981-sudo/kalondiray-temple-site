Naihati Shibtola Temple

Ancient Shiva Temple of Naihati

About Temple

Naihati Shibtola Temple is a sacred Shiva temple located at Naihati (Shibtola/Marotola), Post-Arit, Dist-Paschim Medinipur, Pin-721148. The temple is a place of devotion, peace and spirituality for local devotees. Recently the temple has been newly reconstructed and inaugurated on: 17/06/2024 (English Date) 12/03/1431 (Bangla Date) Devotees gather here during festivals, Shiva Puja and religious events throughout the year.

Temple History

The old temple of Naihati Shibtola was an important spiritual center for the village people for many years. Over time, the temple became aged and later a beautiful new temple was constructed with the support and devotion of local villagers and devotees. Today the temple stands as a symbol of faith, culture and unity among the people of Naihati and nearby villages.
