📜 Temple History

Baba Kalondiroy Shiv Temple is a sacred spiritual place located in Naihati village of Paschim Medinipur, West Bengal. The temple is deeply connected with local Hindu culture, Shiva devotion, Gajan traditions, and Maha Shivratri celebrations. Devotees from nearby villages gather here during annual festivals and special puja ceremonies.
