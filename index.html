📜 Temple History

The sacred legacy of Baba Kalondiroy Shiv Temple spans well over a century, standing as an eternal testament to divine miracles, rich cultural heritage, and the absolute devotion of the local community. Unlike modern temples, the spiritual core of this shrine holds a magnificent Swayambhu Lingam—a self-manifested deity that emerged naturally from the deep womb of the earth. Local generations firmly believe that this sacred stone is a direct, divine portal originating from the mystical realm of Patal Lok, pushing its way into the mortal world to bless and protect humanity.

According to ancient lore passed down by village elders, this heavenly presence was first discovered hidden beneath the rustic shade of a sacred Choi tree. In those early days, it was nothing more than a humble, small clearing where devotees gathered to offer simple prayers. As the divine fame of Baba Kalondiroy spread, the community united to construct a permanent home for the deity. This first historic structure was built using the ancient architectural methods of Bengal—crafted entirely out of traditional Chun-Surki (lime and brick mortar). To honor the Lord, ancient artisans spent years carving intricate, hand-made terracotta motifs and murtis onto its walls, alongside majestic creations of traditional animals like tigers and lions, showcasing a rich era of regional art.

However, as the decades rolled by, the antique structure suffered severe structural decay due to the harsh elements of time, eventually becoming dilapidated and distressed. Seeing their beloved shrine in danger, the residents of two neighboring villages—Naihati and Bhuniarah—came together as a single family. Bound by their shared duty as the rightful custodians of Baba Kalondiroy, the people executed a massive, historic renovation project approximately 30 years ago, breathing new life into the weakening walls and preserving the sacred compound for their children.

Today, history is turning its most magnificent page yet. Driven by a shared divine inspiration and the collective command of the people, the united communities of Naihati and Bhuniarah have taken a monumental resolution. Recognizing that the old structure can no longer accommodate the thousands of pilgrims who gather here, they have decided to reconstruct the entire shrine from its very foundations. This ambitious modern project is currently underway to build a breathtaking, sky-piercing architectural marvel. Once completed, this monumental crown jewel will proudly stand as the grandest and biggest Shiv Temple in the entire Daspur region, carrying the glorious name of Baba Kalondiroy into the next century.

🎭 Festivals & Programs

Baba Kalondiroy Temple comes alive during major festivals like Maha Shivratri, the annual Gajan festival and mela, and the holy month of Shravan, which draws thousands of devotees to pour sacred water on the Swayambhu.
